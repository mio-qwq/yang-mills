# Exact antisymmetric dimension-six classification certificate

Scope: translation-invariant gauge-invariant local polynomial densities in a pure SU(2) connection on Euclidean four-space, engineering dimensions [F]=2,[D]=1. Coefficients are real constants. Equalities of jets incorporate curvature antisymmetry, Bianchi, derivative commutators, and trace identities. Total derivatives have gauge-invariant local currents. On-shell means quotient by the differential ideal generated by J_mu=D_nu F_nu_mu. No global Yang–Mills estimate or quantum assertion follows.

Let W be the full signed permutation group and L=Lambda^2 R^4. The multiplicities of L in dimension-six densities, dimension-five currents, and dimension-four antisymmetric potentials are respectively (11,16,7); on shell they are (6,12,7). The divergence map on equivariant currents has rank 9 off shell and 5 on shell. The curl map from antisymmetric potentials into currents has rank 7 in both cases. Thus the middle complex is exact in this L-isotypic channel, without requiring a general covariant Poincare lemma. The resulting quotient multiplicities are 2 and 1.

## Jet modules and color

Write J_k for independent linear curvature derivatives of order k. A normal/radial gauge identifies these with the Young module (k+1,1), equivalently V tensor Sym^(k+1)V minus Sym^(k+2)V. Thus J_0=L has dimension 6, J_1 dimension20, J_2 dimension45. Equivalent identities are J_1=V tensor L−Lambda^3 V and J_2=Sym²V tensor L−V tensor Lambda³V+Lambda⁴V. Antisymmetric second derivatives are brackets [F,F] and do not supply independent second jets. The derivative Bianchi identities provide the displayed Young relations.

The equation J removes V from J_1. Its first derivatives remove V tensor V minus the scalar from J_2: the missing scalar relation is D_mu J_mu=0, an identity following from antisymmetry and [F_mu_nu,F_mu_nu]=0. Consequently the on-shell jet dimensions are 16 and30.

For SU(2), invariant color tensors of degree2 and3 are respectively the inner product and alternating volume form. At engineering dimensions4,5,6 the gauge-invariant associated graded modules are

Replace J1,J2 by their on-shell modules for the on-shell formulas. These are statements of actual W-character, since finite-group representations split their filtration. In particular, exact averaging over all384 W elements gives multiplicity zero for L in Lambda³J0. This is essential: cubic nonabelian commutator corrections cannot alter a map into this antisymmetric channel. ## Small explicit divergence complex

A W-equivariant antisymmetric tensor is determined by its component12. Its monomials must have indices1 and2 odd and indices3 and4 even. After this sign condition, the stabilizer condition is anti-invariance under (12) and invariance under (34). Apply the unnormalized four-element projector

P = 1 − (12) + (34) − (12)(34).

To span dimension-five currents, project every monomial j_a=F_bc partial_d F_ef with b<c,e<f and the stated six-index parity. To span dimension-four antisymmetric potentials, project K_ab=F_cd F_ef, with a<b,c<d,e<f, use K_ba=−K_ab, and impose the same parity. Color pairing is understood. Sorting and cancelling yields36 projected current candidates and9 projected potential candidates. Their linear dependencies are left intact. This construction exhausts the required equivariant spaces, not a selected ansatz.

Evaluate their polarized quadratic parts on two waves with momenta p,q and polarizations u,v. Put f_bc=p_b u_c−p_c u_b and g_bc=q_b v_c−q_c v_b. Harmless common factors of i are suppressed. The current monomial evaluates to

Its divergence is sum_a(p_a+q_a)j_a. A potential monomial evaluates to K_ab=f_cd g_ef+g_cd f_ef; its curl contributes (p_b+q_b)K_ab to component a and −(p_a+q_a)K_ab to component b.

## Why this is an exact proof, not only a numerical lower bound

The character calculation bounds current dimensions by16 off shell and12 on shell. Seven independent curl images lie in the divergence kernel, because partial_a partial_b K_ab=0 identically for every antisymmetric gauge-invariant K. Therefore divergence ranks are at most16−7=9 and12−7=5. The displayed modular minors give the matching lower bounds9 and5, so both ranks are proved over characteristic zero. Simultaneously the current-span and curl minors verify all necessary independence assertions. Subtracting these ranks from density multiplicities11 and6 gives2 and1.

The only possible nonlinear discrepancy in density divergence is cubic in F. Its L-multiplicity is zero, as verified independently by the character sum, so the quadratic computation determines the full nonabelian divergence in this channel. Curl identities use ordinary derivatives of gauge-invariant expressions and hold without any abelianization. Thus the calculation applies to SU(2), not merely free Maxwell theory.

### Direct check that the cubic antisymmetric channel vanishes

This vanishing does not require trusting the character program. An element of Lambda³(Lambda²V) is a wedge of three distinct edges E_ab of the complete graph on four vertices. In the sign-character sector of E_12, vertices1 and2 must have odd degree and vertices3 and4 even degree. A three-edge simple graph with this degree parity is necessarily a length-three path from1 to2 through3 and4. Thus this sector is two-dimensional, with basis

u=E_13 wedge E_34 wedge E_24,
v=E_14 wedge E_34 wedge E_23.

Both transpositions(12) and(34) act by u→−v and v→−u. But an equivariant image of E_12 must be negated by(12) and fixed by(34), so it is zero. Signed-permutation transitivity proves Hom_W(Lambda²V,Lambda³(Lambda²V))=0. This establishes directly that no nonlinear cubic correction survives in the divergence target considered above.

### Explicit surjectivity of the equation-of-motion jet constraints

Here is an elementary justification of the on-shell character subtractions, independently of dimension counting. At the linearized level write M(A)=Delta A−grad(div A), in spatial dimension d. Every constant current v is realized by A(x)=|x|²v/[2(d−1)], since Delta(|x|²v)=2dv and grad div(|x|²v)=2v. Every trace-free linear current Bx is realized by

A(x)=|x|² [B_sym/(2d)+B_skew/(2(d+2))]x.

Indeed, for any matrix C,

Delta(|x|²Cx)=2(d+2)Cx,
grad div(|x|²Cx)=4C_sym x+2 tr(C)x.

For tr B=0, substitution gives M(A)=Bx. Conversely div M(A)=0, so the linear-current coefficient has zero trace; this is the only compatibility condition. Thus the first curvature derivative constraints surject onto V, and the second curvature derivative constraints surject onto the trace-free V tensor V module, dimension d²−1. At fixed lower nonlinear jets, these top-order equations are affine with exactly these principal linear maps. Their ranks therefore remain4 and15 in dimension4; passing on shell leaves dimensions16 and30, rather than merely formally subtracting characters.
