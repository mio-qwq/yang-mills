# Antisymmetric dimension-six SU(N) operators: quotient dimensions 6/5 and four symmetric-cubic classes

## Scope and statement

Let N≥3 and let A be an su(N)-valued connection on Euclidean four-space. Let Vδ denote the real vector space of constant-coefficient, gauge-invariant polynomial local densities of engineering dimension δ, generated by the curvature F and its covariant derivatives, with [F]=2 and [D]=1. Curvature antisymmetry, the Bianchi identities, derivative commutators and color trace identities are imposed. No explicit coordinates, background tensors other than constant contraction coefficients, matter fields, or inverse derivatives are allowed.

The full hyperoctahedral group W₄ acts on spatial indices. Put L=Λ²R⁴. An antisymmetric tensor operator means an element of Hom_W₄(L,Vδ). Quotient by total derivatives means divergences of gauge-invariant constant-coefficient local polynomial currents. The on-shell quotient additionally imposes the full differential ideal generated by the Yang–Mills equations Jν=DμFμν=0, not just linearized equations.

**Theorem.** At dimension six, the antisymmetric tensor-operator quotient has dimension **6 modulo total derivatives**, and dimension **5 modulo total derivatives and the Yang–Mills differential ideal**, for every N≥3. Four independent classes in either quotient are symmetric-color cubic tensors whose component 12 is

1. d(F₁₂,F₁₂,F₁₂);
2. Σₖ₌₃,₄ [d(F₁₂,F₁ₖ,F₁ₖ)+d(F₁₂,F₂ₖ,F₂ₖ)];
3. d(F₁₂,F₃₄,F₃₄);
4. d(F₁₃,F₂₄,F₃₄)−d(F₁₄,F₂₃,F₃₄).

Here d(X,Y,Z)=−i tr(Sym(XYZ)), with Sym the average over all six orders; any nonzero real normalization is equivalent. Extend these components by signed permutations and antisymmetry. They exhaust the symmetric-color cubic antisymmetric channel.

These are finite local-algebra statements. They do not establish continuum limits, renormalization-group estimates, rotational restoration, global Yang–Mills existence, or a mass gap.

## Proof

### 1. All relevant color tensors

Complexify su(N) to sl_N. The equivariant projection End(Cᴺ)→sl_N, X↦X−tr(X)I/N, extends any invariant multilinear form on sl_N to one on End(Cᴺ). The invariant contraction theorem at tensor degrees two and three, equivalently the Schur–Weyl centralizer description, expresses the extension as a linear combination of permutation contractions. Such contractions are products of traces associated with permutation cycles.

On traceless arguments, terms with singleton cycles vanish. At degree two only tr(XY) survives. At degree three only tr(XYZ) and tr(XZY) survive. Their difference is alternating and proportional to tr(X[Y,Z]); their sum is symmetric by cyclicity. Thus there is one quadratic invariant and, for N≥3, exactly one alternating and one symmetric cubic invariant, with no mixed-symmetry invariant. Paired upper and lower epsilon contractions at N=3 are already permutation contractions and add nothing.

Both cubic invariants are nonzero. The alternating one follows from the nonzero bracket and nondegenerate trace pairing. For the symmetric one take H=diag(i,i,−2i,0,…,0): tr(H³)=6i and d(H,H,H)=6. The symmetric cubic vanishes for su(2), which accounts for the difference from that case.

### 2. Spatial jet modules and equations

Independent linear curvature jets of orders zero, one and two have W₄-modules J₀,J₁,J₂ of dimensions 6,20,45. Normal gauge identifies Jₖ with the Young module (k+1,1), or in characters

Jₖ = V⊗Symᵏ⁺¹V − Symᵏ⁺²V.

On shell J₁ loses V and J₂ loses V⊗V−1, leaving dimensions 16 and 30. These subtractions are actual constraint ranks. For the linearized equation M(A)=ΔA−∇div A in spatial dimension d, every constant current v is realized by A=|x|²v/[2(d−1)]. Every trace-free linear current Bx is realized by

A=|x|²[B_sym/(2d)+B_skew/(2(d+2))]x.

Indeed Δ(|x|²Cx)=2(d+2)Cx and ∇div(|x|²Cx)=4C_sym x+2tr(C)x. The only compatibility is div M(A)=0. At fixed lower nonlinear jets, the highest-order equations are affine with the same surjective principal maps; their ranks remain 4 and 15. This justifies using the full nonlinear equation ideal.

At dimensions four and five only the quadratic color pairing occurs. At dimension six, the relevant modules are therefore

V₄ = Sym²J₀,
V₅ = J₀⊗J₁,
V₆ = (J₀⊗J₂) ⊕ Sym²J₁ ⊕ Λ³J₀ ⊕ Sym³J₀.

For the on-shell characters replace J₁,J₂ by their on-shell versions. The direct-sum formulas describe the associated graded color-degree decomposition and hence the actual W₄ character, because finite-group representations split filtrations. The last summand, symmetric color, is the new summand absent for SU(2).

Exact character averaging over all 384 signed permutations gives the following multiplicities of L:

- Dimension-six densities: 15 off shell, 10 on shell
- Dimension-five vector currents V⊗V₅: 16 off shell, 12 on shell
- Dimension-four antisymmetric potentials Λ²V⊗V₄: 7 in both cases
- Symmetric cubic summand Sym³J₀: 4
- Alternating cubic summand Λ³J₀: 0

The supplied scripts reproduce these counts. There is also a direct explanation for the final vanishing. The sign-character sector of E₁₂ in Λ³(Λ²V) is spanned by the two three-edge paths 1–3–4–2 and 1–4–3–2. The swaps (12) and (34) act identically on that space, whereas the target E₁₂ is negated by (12) and fixed by (34). An equivariant image is therefore zero.

### 3. Exact divergence ranks

The unchanged dimension-five current space has dimension 16 off shell or 12 on shell in this channel. Seven independent curls of gauge-invariant antisymmetric dimension-four potentials have identically zero divergence. Hence divergence ranks are at most 9 and 5.

The included finite certificates give matching lower bounds. They evaluate projected current polynomials on two waves in one fixed color direction. The selected minors are nonzero modulo 1009; explicit null and transverse rational parametrizations lift the on-shell samples to Q(i), with i reducing to 469. Thus they certify characteristic-zero lower bounds. The determinant residues for current, divergence and curl minors are respectively (586,990,585) off shell and (579,149,336) on shell. The checker independently reconstructs every selected entry and computes determinants by integer Bareiss elimination.

These are full nonabelian bounds. A sum of null transverse waves in one fixed color direction solves the nonlinear equations exactly: all commutators vanish. Every member of the full equation ideal vanishes on those tests. For nonlinear corrections, differentiating quadratic color contractions produces only alternating bracket color f, never symmetric color d; the alternating cubic antisymmetric target has multiplicity zero. Thus no hidden symmetric cubic divergence is generated. Equivalently, the quadratic divergence already has the maximum permitted rank 9 or 5, so its kernel is exactly the seven curl directions, whose divergences vanish identically.

The divergence ranks are therefore exactly 9 and 5. Subtracting from density multiplicities gives 15−9=6 and 10−5=5.

### 4. Four cubic representatives and a nonlinear witness

The component-12 stabilizer conditions are: odd parity in indices 1 and 2, even parity in indices 3 and 4, negation under (12), and invariance under (34). The four displayed cubics obey these conditions and have disjoint monomial supports. A complete linear system on the 56 cubic monomials in six curvature components has rank 52. Thus its solution space has dimension four, and the four displayed polynomials give a basis. A checker separately verifies the full-group character multiplicity and this stabilizer system; replacing the minus sign in the fourth polynomial by a plus sign fails the equations.

Their survival in the full quotient has a particularly direct witness. Choose any constant real antisymmetric matrix f and set

Aμ(x)=−½ Σν fμν xν H,

where H=diag(i,i,−2i,0,…,0). Then Fμν=fμνH, DF=0, and J=0 exactly. Every dimension-five gauge-invariant current is quadratic of type F DF and vanishes identically on these connections; so does its divergence. Equation-ideal expressions vanish as well.

The four cubics reduce to four independent ordinary polynomials in the six freely chosen f components, multiplied by d(H,H,H)=6. No nonzero linear combination can therefore be an equation term plus a permitted divergence. This proves independence of the four new classes directly, including on shell. The connections are local polynomial witnesses; no finite-action, compact-support or global boundary condition is asserted or needed. ∎

## Charge-conjugation refinement

Under C(A)=-A^T, the trace pairing and alternating cubic color tensor are even, while the symmetric cubic tensor is odd. All dimension-five currents are even. Divergence and the full equation ideal preserve this involution. Consequently the C-even quotient has dimensions **2 off shell / 1 on shell** for every N>=2; the C-odd quotient has dimensions **4/4** for N>=3 and is zero for N=2.

The Wilson action and Haar measure preserve C. In a C-preserving regulator, renormalization prescription and observable/source sector, rotation counterterms can be chosen C-even, so the four additional odd classes do not themselves enlarge that sector. No assertion about arbitrary C-breaking insertions or selected infinite-volume states follows.
