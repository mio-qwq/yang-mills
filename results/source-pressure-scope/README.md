# Supplementary fixed-cutoff source-pressure scope

[RESULT.md](RESULT.md) audits the logical implication from specified, pinned manuscript source estimates to a fixed-cutoff homogeneous-source differentiability premise. [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md) gives the exact cosh test family and the normal-family repair.

The conclusion is limited: the inspected estimates do not establish H2 for the required general local and reflected-product source directions. This conclusion grants the stated multiscale inputs for purposes of the implication test; it is not an accusation about the authors or a verdict on the full manuscript. The cosh family is an abstract counterexample to a proposed inference, not a Wilson DLR counterexample.

A positive conditional result is included: at fixed cutoff, a common zero-free complex disk for each selected bounded local homogeneous source along an unbounded periodic-volume subsequence implies the corresponding differentiability statement, by Borel–Carathéodory, Montel, and real-pressure convergence. The common disk remains an additional hypothesis. H1 and H2 are local labels explained in [the existence note](../invariant-diagonal-rp/README.md), distinct from the manuscript's numbered hypotheses.

No ordinary straight-periodic accessibility, unconditional H2, continuum limit, or mass gap is proved. No novelty, Lean-verification, or human-peer-review claim is made. The arguments here are analytic written proofs; there is no executable checker purporting to certify them.

`SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` identifies the pinned public files. Downloaded originals, screenshots, and verbatim excerpt files are intentionally excluded. Run `python results/verify_research_2026_10_18.py` from the repository root to verify the hashes and regenerate the finite checks accompanying the other two notes. Detailed geometry output JSON files are not tracked or archived.
